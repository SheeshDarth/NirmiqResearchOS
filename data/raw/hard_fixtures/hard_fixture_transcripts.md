# Hard Fixture Transcripts

These transcripts mirror the synthetic scan and handwriting fixtures used by the hard-document pipeline. They let the generalization benchmark include scanned-PDF and handwriting query categories without requiring local OCR during every broad eval run.

## Scanned Signal Processing Notes

Spectral leakage spreads energy into neighboring frequency bins when the observation window does not contain an integer number of cycles. A tapered window reduces leakage but also widens the main lobe.

Calibration procedure: first remove sensor bias, then collect three reference readings, and finally store the median correction. Frequency resolution equals sample rate divided by sample count.

## Handwritten Lab Note

Before measuring thermal response, zero the probe and wait two minutes for the reference bath to stabilize.

If the trace oscillates, reduce controller gain before repeating the test.
