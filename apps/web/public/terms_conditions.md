# NIRMIQ ResearchOS Terms and Conditions

Last updated: 2026-05-29

## MVP status

NIRMIQ ResearchOS is currently a local-first MVP/prototype. It is provided for research, study, and development use.

## No academic misconduct

Users are responsible for following their institution's academic integrity policies. NIRMIQ can help understand documents, create drafts, and trace citations, but users must review, verify, and author final submissions responsibly.

## No professional advice

NIRMIQ output is not legal, medical, financial, or professional advice. Always verify source material and citations.

## Citation responsibility

NIRMIQ attempts to ground answers in uploaded sources, but users must validate citations and references before using outputs in papers, exams, reports, or publications.

## Local files

Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to ingest and process local files. Do not upload or process documents that violate copyright, privacy, or institutional rules.

## No warranty

This MVP is provided without warranty. It may produce incomplete, incorrect, or poorly grounded outputs. Use it as an assistant, not an authority.
